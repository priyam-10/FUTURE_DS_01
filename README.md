## 🔍 Key Findings

### 🌟 Revenue Performance

* Generated over **$2.30 Million** in total sales.
* Achieved **$286.40 Thousand** in total profit.
* Sales performance varied across different business segments.

### 🌎 Regional Analysis

* West region generated the highest sales revenue.
* South region recorded the lowest sales contribution.
* Significant regional performance differences were observed.

### 🏆 Category Analysis

* Technology emerged as the top-performing category.
* Office Supplies demonstrated strong profitability.
* Furniture generated high revenue but lower profit margins.

### 📦 Product Analysis

Top-performing products contributed a significant portion of overall business revenue, highlighting opportunities for focused marketing and inventory optimization.

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

* Increase investment in Technology products.
* Improve profitability within the Furniture category.
* Replicate successful sales strategies from the West region.
* Prioritize inventory for top-performing products.
* Strengthen sales efforts in lower-performing regions.
