### Future Interns – Data Science & Analytics Internship

A comprehensive sales analytics project focused on uncovering revenue trends, regional performance, customer behavior, category profitability, and top-performing products using Python and Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA).

# 🎯 Project Objective

The objective of this project is to analyze retail sales data and generate actionable business insights by examining:

* Sales Trends
* Regional Performance
* Customer Contributions
* Category Profitability
* Product Performance
* Business Growth Opportunities

# 🔍 Key Findings

* Generated over **$2.30 Million** in total sales.
* Achieved **$286.40 Thousand** in total profit.
* West region recorded the highest sales revenue.
* Technology category delivered the highest profitability.
* Top products significantly contributed to overall revenue.
* Customer and segment analysis revealed valuable business opportunities.

---

# 💡 Recommendations

* Increase focus on Technology products.
* Improve profitability within the Furniture category.
* Replicate successful sales strategies from the West region.
* Prioritize inventory for top-performing products.
* Strengthen marketing efforts in lower-performing regions.
